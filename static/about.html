How it works.

LLMRadar is an independent tracker. No vendor pays to appear, to rank higher, or to have an unflattering number removed.

Where the numbers come from

A scheduled job re-reads each vendor's own published pricing on a fixed interval and writes the result to the database this site reads from. Nothing is entered by hand and forgotten.

Not every vendor is equally scrapeable. Some publish server-rendered pricing pages that can be parsed reliably; others render prices in the browser or block automated readers, so those entries are curated snapshots that get re-checked by hand. Every row tells you which it is.

What the freshness badges mean

  • LIVE — pulled directly from the vendor's pricing page within the last 7 days by the automated job.
  • VERIFIED — a curated value, last confirmed against the vendor page under 30 days ago. Accurate when checked, but not machine-refreshed.
  • STALE — nobody has confirmed this in over 30 days. Treat it as indicative and check the vendor page yourself.

How prices are normalised

All prices are shown per 1 million tokens in USD, which is how most vendors now quote. Where a vendor publishes per-1K pricing it is multiplied up. Cached-input pricing is listed separately because the discount is large and easy to miss — on some models a cache read costs a tenth of a fresh input token.

Figures exclude batch discounts, committed-use agreements, enterprise negotiation and free-tier allowances. The cost calculator on the pricing page makes the same assumption, so treat its output as a ceiling rather than a quote.

What is deliberately not here

No benchmark leaderboard. Quality rankings go stale faster than prices and depend heavily on your prompt, your evals and your task — a single number would be misleading. The "best for" column repeats what the vendor says the model is for, and is labelled as such.

Disclosure

LLMRadar is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq, Mistral or any other provider. Some outbound links may in future carry referral codes; if that happens it will be stated on the link itself and will never affect ordering, inclusion or the numbers shown.

Corrections

If a price here is wrong, it is a bug. The source of truth is always the vendor's own pricing page, linked from every model record in the API at /api/models.
